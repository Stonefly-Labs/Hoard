**Purpose**: Assess completeness, clarity, consistency, and measurability of
F-01's provider-neutral boundaries, explicit composition, and acceptance requirements.
**Created**: 2026-10-20
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)

during PR requirements review, before implementation authorization.
