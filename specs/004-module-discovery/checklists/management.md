# Management Requirements Quality Checklist: Module Capabilities and Management Discovery

**Purpose**: Assess the completeness, clarity, consistency, coverage and
measurability of F-04's protected management discovery, safe disclosure and
consumer presentation requirements, not implementation behavior.
**Created**: 2026-10-21
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)
**Audience / Timing**: Maintainers and management-API/consumer reviewers during
PR requirements review, before implementation authorization.
**Depth / Scope**: Standard; management-read and exposure boundaries, metadata
privacy, safe diagnostics, versioned reads, freshness, action eligibility and
semantic accessibility. Inventory/composition and bounded observation
requirements are covered by the separate [discovery checklist](discovery.md).
No exploit audit, production identity/proxy certification, rendered console or
measured screen accessibility is implied.

**Note**: This custom checklist is generated by the `/speckit-checklist` command based on feature context and requirements.
**Review Ownership**: This checklist is a reviewer-owned requirements-quality review artifact. Mark an item `[x]` only when the reviewer determines the requirements-quality criterion is satisfied.
**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means the criterion has been reviewed and satisfied for requirements quality. It does not mean implementation work is complete.
